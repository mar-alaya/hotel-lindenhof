Our Restaurant

Traditional Austrian cooking

At Hotel Lindenhof, we cook the way people do in the Styrian mountains: with seasonal, regional ingredients and recipes handed down over generations. Our menu brings together Austrian classics such as Frittatensuppe, Wiener Schnitzel and Kaiserschmarrn, along with vegetarian and vegan options for every guest.

After a day on the trails or the slopes, come in, warm up and enjoy a relaxed dinner in our wood-panelled dining room.
