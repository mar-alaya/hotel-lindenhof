Cancellation Policy

The standard cancellation policy at most hotels allows you to cancel free of charge up to 24 or 48 hours before your scheduled arrival time on the date of your booking. If you decide to cancel after this deadline or fail to check in, the usual penalty is a charge equivalent to the cost of the first night´s stay plus taxes. Please note that this benefit is usually associated with flexible rates, as promotional or non-refundable rates do not allow for changes or refunds, and the terms and conditions are always governed by the hotel’s local time zone.
